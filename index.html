04 — Robotics

Robotics and electronics projects

01Assistive robotics

Simple prosthetic hand for people with disabilities

A working prototype of a simple prosthetic hand that helps restore basic hand movement. It reads muscle signals or simple mechanical movements from the user, and the code turns them into precise motor movements that open and close the hand smoothly, at the lowest cost I could manage.

  • Arduino Nano
  • Servo motors
  • Muscle sensors
  • C++
Code repository (soon) Demo video (soon)

02Environment

Smart automatic waste sorting bin

An environmental project that makes recycling easier in public places. The bin has three sections for organic, plastic and paper waste. A microcontroller reads the sensors, an LCD screen guides the user, and servo-driven flaps send each item to the right section.

  • Microcontroller
  • Sensors
  • Servo motors
  • Mechanical routing
